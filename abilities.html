Special Abilities & Weapons

▼ Spatial Awareness (Main Weapon)

Isagi's most distinctive ability. He can perceive and manipulate space on the soccer field in extraordinary ways. This allows him to:

  • Visualize optimal positioning and player movement
  • Predict opponent actions before they happen
  • Find gaps in defensive formations
  • Execute unpredictable passes and shots
  • Read the game at a macro level
▼ Metavision

An advanced evolution of his spatial awareness that allows him to see beyond the immediate field. He can coordinate with teammates by understanding their positioning and intentions, making him an exceptional playmaker and striker.

▼ Direct Shot Technique

Isagi possesses remarkable accuracy and power in his shots. He can:

  • Place shots with extreme precision
  • Shoot with significant power and curve
  • Take shots from unexpected angles
  • Chip shots over goalkeepers
  • Adapt his shooting style mid-game
▼ Ball Control & First Touch

Elite-level ball control allowing Isagi to receive difficult passes, maintain possession under pressure, and execute complex dribbling sequences when needed.

▼ Tactical Intelligence

Beyond his physical abilities, Isagi possesses exceptional soccer intelligence. He can:

  • Analyze opponent tactics quickly
  • Adapt his playstyle in real-time
  • Understand complex team strategies
  • Make split-second decisions
  • Learn from every match and opponent
